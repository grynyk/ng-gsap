Lenis is a separate smooth-scroll library, not part of GSAP. It keeps the page's native scroll, so ScrollTrigger works with it unchanged once two things are wired up: Lenis runs on GSAP's ticker, and every Lenis scroll event calls ScrollTrigger.update. It is running on this page only, for as long as the page is open. Turn it off and scroll again to feel the difference.
